# MAS course - frequent questions

## BDI: desire vs intention
A desire is a state of the world the agent would like to reach; an intention is a desire the agent has committed to pursue with a plan. The difference is the commitment: the agent keeps its intentions until they are achieved, impossible, or no longer relevant (exercise 2 of chapter 1 asks to model this filter).

## Improved BDI loop
The improved loop re-evaluates intentions after each action (reconsideration). It is presented in chapter 1; it is NOT mandatory in the project, but agents of the project must have clear roles and explicit interactions.

## Contract Net protocol
A manager issues a call for proposals, contractors answer with bids, the manager awards the task to the best bid. Useful in the project to route an email to the most competent agent.

## Voting and manipulation
Plurality, Borda, approval and Condorcet methods are covered in part 2 of the manipulation lectures; the exercise asks to model a manipulation of plurality voting.

## Organisational forms
Centralised, hierarchical, peer-to-peer, market-based, coalition and holonic organisations; the choice depends on control, robustness and communication cost.
