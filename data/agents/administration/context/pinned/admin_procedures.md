# Administrative procedures - Universite Exemple

- Mission orders: request on the "Missions" portal at least 3 weeks before departure, with the conference invitation or programme and a cost estimate (transport + accommodation + registration). Contact: missions@univ-example.fr (Mme Petit). Reimbursement: within 30 days after the mission, with original receipts.
- Leave requests: HR portal, validated by the head of department.
- Annual service declaration (teaching hours): to be completed on the OSE application before 30 November 2026.
- Room booking: through the ADE timetable tool or planning@univ-example.fr, at least 48 hours in advance; exam rooms are booked by the student affairs office.
- Grades of the M2 AI must be entered in the APOGEE system; the student affairs office only accepts the official grade sheet.
